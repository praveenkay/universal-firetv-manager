# Compatibility profile validation

1. Update, reboot, and verify a clean stock baseline.
2. Capture a full accessible backup and exact build fingerprint.
3. Inventory packages, services, receivers, settings, Home, input method, and TV inputs.
4. Exclude every critical or unknown package.
5. Snapshot and disable only one candidate for user 0.
6. Test remote buttons, settings, Bluetooth, HDMI/CEC, audio, Appstore, logins, DRM, and playback.
7. Soak for 24 hours with three reboots, sleep/wake, and network reconnection.
8. Restore and prove there are no unexplained package or setting deltas.
9. Repeat: one device is experimental; two matching devices are candidate; three devices or two models in range are required for validated.
10. Review, sign, checksum, publish redacted evidence, and quarantine automatically on assertion failure.
